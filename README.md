# Contractor-Capital-Capacity

## Business Problem: 
A growing contractor may have the operational capability to take on larger projects, yet still be held back by its capital capacity. Working capital, bonding capacity, and access to bank credit determine how much work a contractor can safely pursue, and they increasingly define its competitive edge. This project examines how contracting companies have grown strategically by analyzing their working capital ratios, bonding relationships, and banking relationships, in order to answer three questions:

## Business Questions: 
1. Capital strategy: What strategies allow a contractor to grow its capital capacity at a sustainable rate?
2. Sustained growth: How have successful contractors maintained their financial stability while expanding their capital capacity?
3. Early warning signs: Which financial indicators act as yellow flags when large projects are moving fast, and how should they be prioritized and monitored?
